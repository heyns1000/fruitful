South African payroll calculator

2027 tax year (1 March 2026 – 28 February 2027), SARS tables. Regular monthly salary, annual-equivalent method.

How this is calculated
  • PAYE: (gross − retirement contribution, capped at 27.5% and R350,000 a year) × 12, taxed on the SARS 2027 brackets, minus the primary, secondary and tertiary rebates, ÷ 12.
  • UIF: 1% from the employee and 1% from the employer, on pay up to R17,712 a month (maximum R177.12 each).
  • SDL: 1% of pay, paid by the employer when its annual payroll is over R500,000.
  • Not included: medical scheme tax credits, travel allowances, fringe benefits, bonuses and directors' remuneration. Check final payroll against SARS's own tables.
