The Invention
One block. Every cornice profile. Zero waste in the warehouse.
Block Box™ is a single molded EPS unit containing nearly every cornice geometry in the catalogue — decorative and plain — pre-separated by tear-away bridges. A warehouse worker snaps out exactly the profile needed by hand, no cutting tools, no offcuts, no dedicated SKU per profile.

First deployed February 26, 2026 on a client warehouse floor with the NT108 "Fresh" 110mm profile. Every unit ships under the yellow Value Guarantee mark on its top face.
