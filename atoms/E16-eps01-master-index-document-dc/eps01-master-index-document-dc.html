Full Product Description

What is Cove EPS01? A lightweight ceiling cornice designed to create smooth, decorative transitions between wall and ceiling. Each unit is 1.5 m long, profiled as a cove (85 × 55 × 55 mm), delivering a modern, understated finish without heavy plaster or ornate work.

Material & Installation: Manufactured from 20DV grade expanded polystyrene — a closed-cell foam rated for interior use. The material is easy to cut with a standard mitre box or hot wire cutter and installs in minutes using appropriate cornice adhesive. Once fitted and adhesive fully cured, the white surface can be painted to match ceiling and wall colors, making it flexible for any interior scheme.

Complete Range: CornexConnect supplies Cove EPS01 as a comprehensive array: single loose units, 6-unit consumer packs, 120-unit trade cartons, 6-carton pallets, bulk BlockBox format (168 equivalent units), pallet-stacked BlockBoxes, and single 20ft or 40ft HQ containers in both carton and BlockBox configurations. Every measurement corresponds to a unique SKU and is production-verified and invoiceable.

Sourcing & Support: Built on 15+ years of ceiling cornice design, manufacture, and global distribution. Heyns Schoeman (founder) holds the patent for the world-first 1.5 m EPS CNC-cut cornice system. Full supply chain transparency: from raw material cost through container load pricing, every element is documented and traceable.
