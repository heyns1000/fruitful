Why Smart

Smart doesn't mean a screen.

Every ToyNest™ product is built around one idea: technology should support imaginative, hands-on play — not replace it.

🎯

Screen-optional learning

Core play happens with your hands, not a tablet. The companion app is there for setup and parents — not required for the fun.

🛡️

Safety by design

Child-safe materials, sealed electronics, and parental controls are built in from the first prototype, not bolted on later.

🔄

Grows with play

Firmware updates unlock new activities and difficulty levels over time, so one toy keeps pace as your child does.

🧵

Built to last

Drop-tested housings and long-life batteries mean ToyNest™ toys are made for years of real play, not one holiday season.

📶

Effortless pairing

No accounts to wrangle for kids. Parents pair a toy once from the app; children just pick it up and play.

🔐

Privacy-respecting

No third-party ad tracking, no unnecessary data collection — a toy's job is to play, not to profile.

How It Works

From unboxing to everyday play.

Getting started with a ToyNest™ toy takes three simple steps.

1

Unbox & charge

Every toy ships ready to charge out of the box — no separate parts, no confusing manuals.

2

Pair with the app

Parents scan a code to pair the toy and set preferences like play time and difficulty.

3

Hand it over & play

From here, it's just a toy — kids play, explore, and discover, no ongoing screen time required.
