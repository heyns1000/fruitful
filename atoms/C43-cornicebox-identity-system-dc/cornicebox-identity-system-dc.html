01

The master mark

WHY THIS MARK

The name says box, so the mark is a box. The gold flap lifts to reveal a cornice run inside — product and packaging in one figure, warm enough for a consumer shelf and sober enough for a trade counter.

FIXED ELEMENTS
Carton body — Slate Navy, 10 px corner radius at 290 px artboard
Flap — Gold, fixed 40° rake, flush with the carton's right edge
Cornice wave — Gold, three crests, fully inside the carton walls
Tear-line — dashed, 1 / 8 pattern, always on the navy field
02

Construction & clear space

Built on a 1x unit grid. Carton is 5x wide × 3x tall; the flap rises exactly 1x and steps 1x inward. Never redraw by eye — scale the master file.

Clear space equals 1x on every side — measured from the carton body, not the flap tip. No type, rules, barcodes or competing logos inside that field.

03

Lockups

A · HORIZONTAL — PRIMARY

Default for headers, invoices, shelf talkers and anything wider than tall. Mark height equals the two-line wordmark height.

B · STACKED — SQUARE FORMATS

Pack fronts, social avatars, stamps and van door panels. Descriptor rule may be dropped below 40 mm reproduction.

C · REVERSED — NAVY FIELD

On navy the carton inverts to gold and the flap to white — never leave the carton navy-on-navy with an outline.

D · SMALL MARKS

Left to right: app / favicon badge, outline for embossing and vinyl, single-colour for stamps and one-plate print. The tear-line drops out below 32 px.

05

Minimum sizes

40 mm — FULL MARK
20 mm — TEAR-LINE DROPS
16 PX — BADGE ONLY

Below 40 mm the tear-line is removed and the wave weight increases one step. Below 20 mm use the badge; never shrink the full lockup further.

06

Misuse

✕ RECOLOURED

Navy carton and gold flap are fixed.

✕ ROTATED

The carton always sits level.

✕ RE-PROPORTIONED

5×3 carton ratio never changes.

✕ WAVE BREAKING OUT

Three crests, always inside the walls.

07

Retail applications

CONSUMER PACK — FRONT

Horizontal lockup top-left, profile name as the loudest element, gold block reserved for the price and count.

TRADE CARTON — SIDE

Reversed lockup on navy; count in Fredoka, all dimensions and codes in DM Mono gold.

SHELF STRIP & CARD

Gold strip carries name plus badge only. Stationery uses the horizontal lockup with a DM Mono role line.
