Welcome

Glad you're here. Here's what we're building.

In plain terms, no jargon: Block Box™ is a single moulded packaging block that contains the product inside it — the packaging and the product are the same piece of material. Nothing extra to throw away.
 

What It Is

One block. No separate box.

Most products ship inside a box, wrapped in packaging that gets thrown away the moment it arrives. Block Box™ removes that step entirely — the EPS block itself is the shipping unit and the product, cut to size, ready to use, patent-pending. It's a simple idea that happens to cut cost and waste at the same time.
 

Why It Matters

Roughly 93% lower packaging cost. 94% less waste.

Those two numbers are the whole pitch, really. Everyone in the chain — factories, retailers, and the person who opens the box at home — ends up with less to deal with, not more. We think that's what good design is supposed to do: disappear into being obviously better.
