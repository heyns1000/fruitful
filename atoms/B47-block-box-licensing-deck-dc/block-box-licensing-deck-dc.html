02 — The Innovation
The Block Box System
Multiple cornice profiles are hot-wire cut from a single EPS block and held together by tear-away bridges — thin connective ribs that keep the block structurally whole through shipping, then let go by hand at the install site.
94%
less packaging material than boxes and wrap
40–60%
lower shipping cost per shipment
100%
recyclable, mono-material construction
03
02 — The Innovation
How It Works
01
Cut & Bridge
CNC hot-wire cuts 5–8 profiles into one EPS block, joined by tear-away bridges.
02
Ship as One Block
No cartons, no wrap. The block stacks and palletizes on its own structure.
03
Tear Away by Hand
Bridges release with 2–5kg of manual force. No tools, no training, under 30 seconds.
