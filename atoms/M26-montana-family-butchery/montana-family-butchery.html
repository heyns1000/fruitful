Honoring the Old Ways: The 'Fingers' Cut

This is the essence of our butchery. The 'fingers' (hand-cut portioning) method ensures minimum waste and maximum flavor, done the old-fashioned way, just like Oom and Tannie remember.

Why 'Fingers'?

  • Precision: Every portion is checked by eye and hand, not just by weight.
  • Flavor: The cut follows the natural muscle fiber, preserving juiciness.
  • Respect: It honors the traditional white-coute technique passed down through generations.

Quick Answers to Your Questions

Q: What is 'white coute' butchery?

A: It refers to a traditional, clean, and highly skilled method of preparing meat, often associated with exceptional hygiene and precision.

Q: What does WildButcher.faa.zone mean?

A: It signifies our commitment to the FAA (Future African Agriculture) Wildlife & Habitat protocols, ensuring our chain adheres to global sustainability best practices.

Q: Do you deliver nationally?
