01 — The Problem
Cornice ships in a box it doesn't need.
Decorative cornice and trim profiles are lightweight foam — cheap to make, expensive to move. Every profile is wrapped, boxed, and padded separately, so a pallet of product is mostly cardboard and plastic film by weight and volume. That packaging is discarded within minutes of arrival, and the labour to unpack, sort, and dispose of it is a hidden cost on every order.
02 — The Mechanism
One block. Every profile. Torn out by hand.
Block Box is a single EPS block containing multiple cornice profiles — decorative and plain — separated by tear-away bridges. A warehouse worker or installer snaps out exactly the profile needed, by hand, in under 30 seconds, with no tools and 2–5kg of force. The block itself is the shipping container; there is no secondary packaging to remove.
