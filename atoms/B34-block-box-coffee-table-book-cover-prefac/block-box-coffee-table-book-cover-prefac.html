Preface
Why the box had to disappear.

Every piece of decorative cornice ever sold has arrived the same way: wrapped, boxed, padded, and thrown away within minutes of reaching site. The product was foam. The packaging was also foam. Nobody questioned it, because nobody had separated the two ideas.

Block Box™ starts from a different premise — that the packaging and the product could be the same object. A single moulded EPS block, engineered with tear-away bridges between finished profiles, ships as its own container and separates by hand into exactly what a warehouse needs, in under thirty seconds, with no tools and no offcuts.

This book documents that idea end to end: the mechanism, the physics, the first commercial delivery, and the standard it proposes for how foam building products should move through the world from here. It is written for attorneys, factories, licensees, and retailers evaluating the invention — and for anyone who wants to understand why the box, in this one case, simply wasn't necessary.
