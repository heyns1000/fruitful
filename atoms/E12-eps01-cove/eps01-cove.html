Part 1 — Logo System & Brand Mark

Clearspace, Minimum Size & Correct Application

Logo Variants & Scale Rules
Primary Mark (shown above): 90px minimum digital, 18mm minimum print. Hero packaging and digital headers only.

Clearspace & Contrast (Part 1.1–1.3)
Clearspace unit: Equals the full height of the tear-wave cue. No text, image, edge, or competing mark may enter this zone on all sides.

Background: Mark sits on Deep Navy (#0E2148), Value Yellow (#F5C518), Warm Paper (#EEE6D6), or true white only. Minimum contrast ratio 4.5:1 required.

Approved reproduction: Full-color on Warm Paper; reversed (yellow-on-navy) on Deep Navy; one-color navy or yellow for foil, emboss, thermal, or corrugate runs where full color is not production-viable.

Part 3 — Typography & Hierarchy

Fredoka & Archivo Locked Type Rules

Fredoka
Primary — wordmark, headlines, packaging front copy. Rounded, warm, consumer-first. Never used for body copy or legal text.
Archivo
Secondary — body copy, spec sheets, web UI, legal/technical documents. Neutral, legible at small size, never for wordmark.
Type Roles (locked): Brand display (Fredoka Bold) — wordmark and headlines only, never smaller than 24px/18pt. Packaging display (Fredoka SemiBold) — front-panel product name. UI/body (Archivo Medium/Regular) — web, app, all running text. Technical/spec (Archivo Regular, mono figures) — dimensions, part numbers. Legal/regulatory (Archivo Regular, minimum 6pt/8px) — compliance copy, always Navy on light or white on Navy, never Yellow (fails contrast at small size). Product name title case always ("Block Box," never "BLOCKBOX"). Labels uppercase Archivo Bold with +0.06–0.12em tracking. Body copy sentence case, no forced tracking. Justified text forbidden — ragged-right only.
